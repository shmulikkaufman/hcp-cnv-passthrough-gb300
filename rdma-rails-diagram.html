Each wire is an independent RDMA stream (no bond/LAG/ECMP). NUMA-1 rails carry ~0.8 µs cross-socket DMA penalty, which is why their full-duplex throughput settles lower. The port-1 PFs — a second 400G plane per GPU for dual-plane 800G — are idle (deferred profile change). Guest test networking (192.0.2.x /30s) and host PF MTU 9000 are runtime-only lab settings.
